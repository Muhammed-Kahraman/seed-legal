Seed: Support

Seed: You Are the AI · iPhone

Questions, bugs, or a scene that read wrong? Write to support@subtillo.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the scene or ending you were on.

FAQ

Is my progress saved? Yes, after every choice, on your device.

How do I change the language? Title screen → Settings → Language.

How many endings are there? Seven, one of them hidden. The Endings screen shows which ones you have found.
